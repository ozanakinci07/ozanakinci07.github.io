PRIVACY POLICY

Passing Lane · Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Passing Lane game (“the app”) uses and why. The app is made by Mustafa Ozan Akıncı (“we”).

Your game progress

Completed matches, trophies, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any servers and we never receive this information. No account is needed to play.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how ads are viewed and tapped.

  • On iPhone, the app asks for your permission before any tracking. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using data from other apps and websites.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent message. You can change your choice at any time from the app’s settings.
  • You can also limit ad tracking in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google’s use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your Game Center nickname and your season trophy count are shared with Apple to show the leaderboard. Apple handles this under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store it.

Children

The app is rated 4+ but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
