PASSING LANE

Football Puzzle

Draw one unbroken pass from your keeper to your striker. Pass to every numbered player in order, cover the whole pitch, beat the defenders and score.

HOW TO PLAY

  • 1Start with the keeper. Number 1 begins every move.
  • Pass in order. Draw the ball to 2, 3, 4 and on, square by square.
  • #Cover the whole pitch. Every square exactly once. Leave one empty and the flag goes up for offside.
  • Beat the defenders. No pass gets through a blue block.
  • 11Score. The route ends at number 11, the striker in yellow.

FIXTURES

60 seasons of 30 matches. Pitches grow and defences tighten as you climb. Win a full season to lift its trophy and post it on the Game Center leaderboard.

DAILY MATCH

A fresh 7×7 puzzle every day, the same for every player in the world. Come back daily and keep your streak alive.

SLOT

Pull the slot for a random pitch size and a random clock, then race to score before the final whistle.
