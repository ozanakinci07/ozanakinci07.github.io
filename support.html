Frequently asked

How do I play?

Start at number 1, pass to the numbered players in order, go through every square exactly once and finish at number 11. Blue blocks are defenders: no pass goes between them.

I’m stuck on a match.

Tap Hint. You have 3 per match, and each one shows the next correct pass. In Slot mode a hint costs 5 seconds.

The commentator said “offside”.

You reached number 11 with empty squares left. Every square must be covered before the goal counts, so the move restarts from the keeper.

What is the Daily Match?

A new 7×7 puzzle every day, the same for every player. Play it each day to grow your streak.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your iPhone’s language. To change it for Passing Lane only: Settings › Passing Lane › Language.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. If you sign in to Game Center, your season trophies also appear on the leaderboard.

Can I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Yes. Use the three switches at the top of the main menu.

How do I stop personalised ads?

Choose “Ask App Not to Track” when asked, or turn off tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. You will still see ads, but they won’t be personalised.
